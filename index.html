Case study “Match Talents” · To-Be process

Match Talents Platform — concept mockup

Today a staffing order travels by e-mail: Sales copies the client's request into the CRM, HR writes to every talent and waits for replies, then e-mails the client and waits again. Most of the cycle time is waiting. On the platform, the client and the talents answer directly, and HR sees every order's status in one place.

    Disclaimer

    This is a concept mockup, not a product. It shows a proposed process redesign from a university case study about the staffing agency easystaff.

    • All people, clients and addresses are fictional (e.g. “Demo Client GmbH”, “Talent A (demo)”).
    • There is no backend, login, e-mail or push notification. Everything is stored in your browser only.
    • Talents are selected by HR by hand, as in the To-Be model — there is no matching algorithm.
    • Not affiliated with easystaff; no easystaff logo, colours or screenshots are used.

    How to click through
