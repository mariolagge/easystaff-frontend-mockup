Match Talents Platform

Today a staffing order travels by e-mail: Sales copies the client's request into the CRM, HR writes to every talent and waits for replies, then e-mails the client and waits again. Most of the cycle time is waiting. On the platform, the client and the talents answer directly, and HR sees every order's status in one place.
